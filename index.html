Arrow Grid — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Arrow Grid ("the game") is made by Atish Agrawal, an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the developer does not collect, store on its own servers, or share any personal data. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games data as described below.

What the game collects

Arrow Grid supports optional sign-in with Google Play Games Services. The game works fully without signing in. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own.

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and best times to Google Play Games Services so they can appear on your achievements list and on leaderboards. This uses your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar), which Google provides to the game. The developer does not receive or store this data on its own servers; Google processes it under the Google Play Games Services terms and the Google Privacy Policy. The Play Games SDK may also send diagnostics and analytics data to Google to keep the service stable.

Data kept on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, hints used, streaks and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this save in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is handled by Google, not by the developer, and you control it in your device settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your device after you stop playing (a few hours apart, never at night) to let you know your next puzzle is waiting. Reminders are created and shown entirely on the device; no notification data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Third parties

The game includes the Google Play Games Services SDK. It includes no advertising or tracking software. When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's Privacy Policy, which the developer does not control.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline or sign out of Play Games at any time. You can view and delete your Play Games data (profile, achievements and scores) in the Play Games app or in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com. Leaderboard visibility follows your Play Games profile visibility setting (friends only, everyone, or only you).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that uses personal data (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
