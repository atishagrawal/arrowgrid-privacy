Arrow Grid — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Arrow Grid ("the game") is made by Atish Agrawal, an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the developer does not collect, store on its own servers, or share any personal data. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games data as described below.

What the game collects

Arrow Grid supports optional sign-in with Google Play Games Services. The game works fully without signing in. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own.

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and best times to Google Play Games Services so they can appear on your achievements list and on leaderboards. This uses your Play Games profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar), which Google provides to the game. The developer does not receive or store this data on its own servers; Google processes it under the Google Play Games Services terms and the Google Privacy Policy. The Play Games SDK may also send diagnostics and analytics data to Google to keep the service stable.

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game also keeps a copy of your game progress (levels completed, best moves, hints used, streaks, records, weekly cup standing and settings) in Google Play Games Saved Games, so your progress can be restored on another device or after you reinstall the game. Saved Games stores this copy with your Google Account; the developer does not receive it or store it on its own servers. If you don't sign in, nothing is saved this way.

Your game progress

Your game progress is saved on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the copy on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in Play Games Saved Games as described above. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this save in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is handled by Google, not by the developer, and you control it in your device settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your device after you stop playing (a few hours apart, never at night) to let you know your next puzzle is waiting. Reminders are created and shown entirely on the device; no notification data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Third parties

The game includes the Google Play Games Services SDK. It includes no advertising or tracking software. When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's Privacy Policy, which the developer does not control.

Your choices and deletion

How to delete your Arrow Grid data: Arrow Grid's online data (your achievements, leaderboard scores and the progress saved with Play Games Saved Games) is stored by Google Play Games, not by the developer. To delete it in the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon at the top right, then tap Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings. Under "Your Data", tap Delete profile & game data; under "Delete individual game data", find Arrow Grid and tap Delete. On the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data; under "Delete individual game data", find Arrow Grid and click Delete. The same page also lets you delete your whole Play Games profile and all of your Play Games data. This deletes your Arrow Grid achievements, leaderboard scores and the game's link to your Play Games profile. Saved Games keeps its copy in a hidden area of your Google Drive; to remove only that copy, open Google Drive settings on the web, choose Manage apps, find Arrow Grid, then Options > Delete hidden app data. If you later sign in to Play Games again, the game re-sends the achievements and progress recorded on your device. The developer keeps no copy of this data, so nothing is retained by the developer after deletion; Google's own retention follows the Google Privacy Policy. The copy of your progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If you need help, email the contact address below.

Signing in to Play Games is optional and the game works fully without it. You can view and delete your Play Games data (profile, achievements, scores and saved progress) in your Play Games profile settings and Google Drive, as described above. Leaderboard visibility follows your Play Games profile visibility setting (friends only, everyone, or only you).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that uses personal data (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
