Arrow Grid — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Arrow Grid ("the game") is made by Atish Agrawal, an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no online features. It never sends any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

Data kept on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, hints used, streaks and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this save in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is handled by Google, not by the developer, and you control it in your device settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your device after you stop playing (a few hours apart, never at night) to let you know your next puzzle is waiting. Reminders are created and shown entirely on the device; no notification data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's settings at any time.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's Privacy Policy, which the developer does not control.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that uses personal data (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
